Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Website: simonchat.ai

This policy applies to Simo, an AI-powered platform for chat, website building, image design, video creation, writing, and related services.

1. Introduction

Simo is an AI-powered service operated by U.S. Publications, Inc. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you use simonchat.ai and its related services.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Simo, we may process account details such as your name, email address, account identifiers and sign-in information; prompts, chats, saved websites, images, books, videos, uploads and other project content; subscription and credit purchase records; and device, browser, IP address, logs and usage information.

3. How we use information

We use information to operate accounts and sign-in, provide AI chat and creation tools, store and retrieve projects, deliver subscription and credit services, detect abuse, troubleshoot problems, provide support, meet legal obligations and maintain the security and reliability of Simo.

4. AI content processing

To provide requested AI features, Simo may send prompts, uploaded files, generated material and relevant context to third-party AI processing providers. Their processing is governed by the agreements and settings applicable to those services. Please avoid including highly sensitive information unless necessary. We will provide a more specific disclosure about whether prompts or other content may be used for model training after our provider settings and agreements are verified.

You retain the rights you hold in content you submit. Saving a project to your account does not by itself make it public. If you deliberately publish a website or share a publicly accessible link, others may see the published material. The rights and restrictions that apply to AI-generated outputs are addressed separately in applicable terms and law.

6. Payments

Simo uses payment-processing services, including Stripe, for subscriptions and credit purchases. Simo may receive payment status, customer or transaction identifiers, subscription details and limited billing information. Payment processors handle payment-card information under their own policies.

7. Sharing and disclosures

We may share information with service providers supporting AI processing, hosting, authentication, payment processing, security and technical operations, to the extent needed to provide services. Cookies and similar technology may be used for sign-in sessions, preferences, security, functionality and site performance. Any analytics or nonessential advertising technology, together with applicable choices or consent mechanisms, must be described according to what is actually deployed.

9. Storage and retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested services, maintain accounts, satisfy applicable legal duties, address disputes and protect Simo. 10. Security

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restrictions or objections, or to opt out of certain processing. Applicable rights and request procedures vary by jurisdiction. Please use the privacy contact listed below to submit a request.
